# Gates-of-WarFighter

A standalone modern-warfare total conversion for *Call to Arms: Gates of Hell*.

Built specifically for Gates of Hell, the project combines large-scale strategic conquest with real-time tactical battles fought using the soldiers, weapons, vehicles, models, and textures of the original WarFighter Modification.

Rather than requiring WarFighter, Code:X, West81, or any other content mod, Gates of Warfighter is being constructed as a completely self-contained experience. Authorized WarFighter assets will be ported into a new Gates of Hell foundation, while factions, national rosters, research trees, campaign progression, AI systems, and strategic mechanics are rebuilt specifically for this project.

Players will choose a modern nation, develop its military, research new capabilities, recruit authentic formations, expand across a persistent operational map, and personally command the battles that determine control of each region.

The strategic campaign is based on the same overmap concept developed for Gates of Code:X, but the underlying mod is independent and designed from the beginning around WarFighter’s modern military assets and faction identities.

WarFighter-derived models and textures are used with the express permission of Yuri. Full credit will be maintained for Yuri and the original WarFighter contributors. Gates of Warfighter is a new Gates of Hell project and not a direct migration of WarFighter’s original code or campaign implementation.
